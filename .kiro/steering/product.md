# Product: Dial Tone from the Grave

Kiroween 2026 entry, Resurrection category. Deadline 4 Dec 2026, 2pm PT.

## The idea
Two dead technologies brought back together:
- the rotary phone
- the printed phone book (white pages for people, yellow-style pages for businesses) and 1023 directory enquiries

You dial a haunted rotary phone. A ghost operator picks up, listens to what you need, and the results come back laid out on a cursed phone book page. The old book could only find what you already knew the name of. This one understands "someone to fix my geyser tonight in Edenvale".

## Who it is for
- Anyone who grew up with a rotary phone and a phone book (the nostalgia hook)
- Judges: they need to get it in 10 seconds and play with it in 2 minutes

## Halloween angle
- Haunted switchboard: each number connects to a different ghost operator with its own personality
- Wrong numbers give static, crackle and a whisper
- Phone book pages are yellowed, stained, candle-lit
- Hidden "spirit listings" of fake ghost businesses as an easter egg
- Tagline idea: "In 1998, the line went dead. Tonight, someone picked up."

## Rules we must stick to
- No real brand names or logos of any directory, telecom or phone company anywhere in the UI, repo, seed data or video. Use our own names and look.
- No unlicensed music. Sound effects must be generated or royalty free.
- Business listings are seeded demo data, not scraped. No real people in the white pages, only invented names.
- No AWS credits or AWS funded hosting (sponsor support disqualifies).
- App must stay live and free for judges until 29 Jan 2027.
